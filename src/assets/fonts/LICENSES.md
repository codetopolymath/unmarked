# Font licences

All three families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).
Files are the Latin subsets served by Google Fonts, bundled locally so the prototype makes no third-party requests.

| Family | Files | Upstream |
|---|---|---|
| Instrument Serif | `instrument-serif-400.woff2`, `instrument-serif-400-italic.woff2` | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Serif |
| Hanken Grotesk | `hanken-grotesk-var.woff2` (variable, 400 to 700) | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Hanken+Grotesk |
| IBM Plex Mono | `plex-mono-400.woff2`, `plex-mono-500.woff2` | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Mono |

Latin only: titles in other scripts fall back to the system serif or sans, which the prototype's non-Latin mock record exercises on purpose.
